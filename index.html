Jana Jobs

Jana Jobs is a private tool used by one person to collect public job ads and prepare job application drafts in Google Drive.

Privacy policy

The app asks only for the Google Drive "drive.file" permission. With it, the app can see and edit only the files it created itself (one folder, one spreadsheet and the documents inside that folder). It cannot see any other file in your Google Drive.

No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. Data stays in the owner's Google Drive. Access can be removed at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
